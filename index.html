Privacy Policy — Kituvo – QR & Barcode Scanner

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Kituvo – QR & Barcode Scanner ("Kituvo", "the app", "we", "our") is designed to scan QR codes and barcodes and to create QR codes while keeping the core processing on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Kituvo does not require an account and does not collect or upload personal information to our servers.

Kituvo does not collect:

  • Names or contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • QR code or barcode contents
  • Scanned URLs, text, Wi-Fi information, phone numbers, emails, or other scan results
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers in the ad-free version

2. Camera Permission

Kituvo requests access to the device camera so that you can scan QR codes and barcodes.

Camera frames are processed locally on the device for scanning. Camera content is not uploaded to Kituvo servers.

You can deny or revoke camera permission through Android settings. Scanning with the camera will not work while the permission is disabled.

3. Photos and Gallery

If you choose to scan a QR code or barcode from an image, Kituvo may request access to the selected image through Android's system image or file picker.

The selected image is processed locally for scanning and is not uploaded to Kituvo servers.

4. Scan History

Scan results may be stored locally on your device so that you can view, copy, share, or delete them.

Kituvo does not synchronize scan history with a cloud service or Kituvo servers.

5. QR Code Creation

Information entered to create a QR code is processed locally on your device.

If you choose to save or share a generated QR code, Android's storage and sharing mechanisms are used according to the action you select.

Kituvo does not upload generated QR codes or their contents to Kituvo servers.

6. Internet Access

The core QR and barcode scanning functionality is designed to work locally and does not require sending scan data to a remote server.

Actions that you explicitly initiate, such as opening a scanned URL or sharing content, may use the internet or other applications. Those actions are governed by the privacy policies of the services you choose to use.

7. Third-Party Services

The V1.0 version of Kituvo is designed without advertising and without a Kituvo cloud backend.

The app may use Android and Google libraries required for camera and barcode-scanning functionality. These libraries process the required data according to their respective technical behavior and privacy policies.

Kituvo does not use these libraries to create a Kituvo account or upload your scan history to Kituvo servers.

8. Data Security

Because the core scanning and QR-generation functions operate locally, scan data does not need to be transmitted to Kituvo servers.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any files or scan history stored on it.

9. Children's Privacy

Kituvo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL, with a revised effective date.
